# Region rules (defaults, always confirm with the user)

| Region | Photo | Personal data | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US / Canada | No | No age, marital status, nationality | 1 page, 2 for senior | "Resume" |
| UK / Ireland | No | Minimal | 2 pages | "CV" |
| EU (ES, FR, IT...) | Optional, check norm | Nationality sometimes; avoid sensitive data (GDPR minimisation) | 1-2 pages | Europass is an option |
| DACH | Common | Often DOB, nationality | 2 pages | Include references to certificates |
| LatAm | Varies | Nationality common | 1-2 pages | Follow local convention |

Never include sensitive data (health, religion, political views). Norms shift: ask the user for the target country.
